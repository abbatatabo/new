# Install on Android (without Google Play)

1. **Download the APK** to your phone:
   `releases/android/the-quran-journey-1.0.0.apk` from this repository (branch
   `claude/quran-journey-app-zc46l8`). It is about 64 MB and works on Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer.
   Alternatively, copy it from your computer to the phone by USB or cloud storage.
2. **Open the file** from your Downloads or Files app.
3. If Android asks, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for the app you opened it with (Chrome or Files).
   Then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Google Play Protect may show "App scanned" or "Unknown developer". Choose **Install anyway**. The app is
   signed with its own release key, not a Play Store key.
5. Open **The Quran Journey**, follow the five short onboarding screens, and allow notifications if you would
   like reminders.

## Add the widgets

Long-press an empty area of the home screen ▸ **Widgets** ▸ **Quran Journey**, then choose:

* **Verse** (2×2): a verse from your current study, its reference and your progress.
* **Today's study** (4×2): the current study, today's progress and your next session.
* **Journey** (4×4): today's sessions, your progress and a quick way to continue.

Tap a widget to open the current study. Open the app once first so the widgets have data. Widgets refresh
when you use the app, at each session time, just after midnight, and every 30 minutes as the system allows.

## Getting reminders on time

* Allow notifications when asked. You can also do this later under *You ▸ Reminders ▸ Allow reminders*.
* Optional: turn on *You ▸ Reminders ▸ Precise timing* and allow "Alarms & reminders". Without this,
  Android may deliver reminders a few minutes late.
* Some manufacturers' battery savers (Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus, Samsung and others) delay or block
  scheduled notifications. If reminders don't arrive, set the app's battery usage to **Unrestricted**.
* Reminders are re-armed after a reboot and after an app update.

## Updating later

Install a newer APK over the existing app. Your progress, notes and bookmarks are kept. Android only accepts
an update signed with the **same key**; see [RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md).
